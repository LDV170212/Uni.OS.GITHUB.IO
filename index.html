WHY I BUILT UNIOS

Built by a student, for students.

I'm Lucas, a Sport Business Management student at Leeds Beckett University.

I started building UniOS after seeing how fragmented university life can be — deadlines in one place, grades somewhere else, notes somewhere else again.

UniOS is my attempt to bring those pieces together into one simple student workspace.
